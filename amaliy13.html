1Mashg‘ulot maqsadi

\(1/C^2-V\) grafigidan kirishma konsentratsiyasi va profilini, kontakt potensiallar farqini hamda maksimal maydonni aniqlash.

Nazariy asos: 3-ma’ruza. Ta’lim natijalari: TN4, TN10, TN13, TN17.

Kerakli vositalarKalkulyator yoki Python (Colab), ngspice/LTspice, darslikdagi interaktiv model, tegishli datasheet.

2Qisqacha nazariy asos

Bir tomonlama o‘tish uchun \(\dfrac{1}{C^2}=\dfrac{2(V_{bi}-V)}{q\varepsilon_sN_dA^2}\). Chiziq qiyaligi \(N_d\) ni, absissa bilan kesishishi \(V_{bi}\) ni beradi. Notekis profil uchun (chuqurlik \(x=\varepsilon_sA/C\)):

$$N(x)=\frac{2}{q\varepsilon_sA^2}\left[\frac{d(1/C^2)}{dV}\right]^{-1}$$

Maksimal maydon: \(E_{max}=\sqrt{\dfrac{2qN_d(V_{bi}-V)}{\varepsilon_s}}=\dfrac{2(V_{bi}-V)}{W}\).

3Ishlangan masalalar

1-masala. Konsentratsiyani qiyalikdan topish

Shart. \(A=10^{-3}\) sm² bo‘lgan p⁺-n diod uchun \(|d(1/C^2)/dV|=1{,}205\cdot10^{21}\) F⁻²V⁻¹, chiziq \(V=0{,}8\) V da absissani kesadi. \(N_d\), \(V_{bi}\) = ?

Yechim

\(N_d=\dfrac{2}{1{,}602\cdot10^{-19}\cdot1{,}036\cdot10^{-12}\cdot10^{-6}\cdot1{,}205\cdot10^{21}}\approx1{,}0\cdot10^{16}\) sm⁻³; \(V_{bi}=0{,}8\) V.

2-masala. Maksimal maydon

Shart. Shu diodda \(V=-5\) V. \(E_{max}\) = ?

Yechim

\(E_{max}=\sqrt{2\cdot1{,}602\cdot10^{-19}\cdot10^{16}\cdot5{,}8/1{,}036\cdot10^{-12}}\approx1{,}34\cdot10^5\) V/sm — Si teshilish maydonidan (~3·10⁵) kichik.

4Darsda mustaqil yechish uchun masalalar

  1. \(V=-1\) V da C = 25 pF, \(V=-3\) V da C = 17 pF (\(A=10^{-3}\) sm²). \(N_d\) = ?
    Javob

    ≈ \(1{,}3\cdot10^{16}\) sm⁻³ (qiyalikdan)

  2. Shu o‘lchovlar uchun profil chuqurligi \(x=\varepsilon_sA/C\) = ?
    Javob

    0,41 va 0,61 mkm

  3. \(N_d=10^{15}\) sm⁻³, \(E_{cr}=3\cdot10^5\) V/sm. Teshilish kuchlanishi \(V_{BR}\approx\varepsilon_sE_{cr}^2/(2qN_d)\) = ?
    Javob

    ≈ 291 V

  4. Simulyatorda \(N_d\) ni 10 marta oshirib, \(E_{max}\) va \(W\) qanday o‘zgarishini tekshiring.
    Javob

    E_max ≈ √10 marta ortadi, W ≈ √10 marta kamayadi

6Kompyuterda modellashtirish

Python (Colab/Jupyter) — nusxalab ishga tushiring va natijani qo‘lda hisob bilan solishtiring (TN6, TN16, TN17).

import numpy as np
q, eps, A = 1.602e-19, 1.036e-12, 1e-3
V = np.array([-1, -3]); C = np.array([25e-12, 17e-12])
slope = np.diff(1/C**2)/np.diff(V)
Nd = 2/(q*eps*A**2*abs(slope[0]))
x = eps*A/C
print(f"N_d = {Nd:.2e} cm^-3, x = {x*1e4} um")
