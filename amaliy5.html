1Mashg‘ulot maqsadi

Zatvor oksidining teshilishi, stok ko‘chkisi, o‘tib ketish (punch-through) hodisalarini va haroratning MOSFET parametrlariga ta’sirini hisoblash.

Nazariy asos: 11-ma’ruza. Ta’lim natijalari: TN8, TN15, TN19.

Kerakli vositalarKalkulyator yoki Python (Colab), ngspice/LTspice, darslikdagi interaktiv model, tegishli datasheet.

2Qisqacha nazariy asos

Teshilish turlari: (1) zatvor oksidining teshilishi — \(E_{bd}\approx10\) MV/sm (SiO₂), \(V_{bd}\approx E_{bd}d_{ox}\); vaqtga bog‘liq teshilish (TDDB); (2) stok o‘tishining ko‘chkisimon teshilishi va parazit BT tufayli “snapback”; (3) o‘tib ketish — stok va istok kambag‘allashgan qatlamlari tutashadi. Haroratning ta’siri: \(\mu\propto T^{-1{,}5}\), \(dV_T/dT\approx-1\ldots-3\) mV/K. Shu sababli kichik \(V_{GS}\) da tok harorat bilan ortadi, katta \(V_{GS}\) da kamayadi; oraliqda harorat koeffitsiyenti nol nuqtasi (ZTC) bor.

3Ishlangan masalalar

1-masala. Oksid teshilishi

Shart. \(d_{ox}=5\) nm, \(E_{bd}=10\) MV/sm. \(V_{bd}\) = ? Ishonchlilik uchun maydon 5 MV/sm dan oshmasligi kerak bo‘lsa, maksimal \(V_{GS}\) = ?

Yechim

\(V_{bd}=10^7\cdot5\cdot10^{-7}=5\) V; ruxsat etilgan \(V_{GS}\le2{,}5\) V.

2-masala. Harorat ta’siri

Shart. \(k(300\,\text{K})=4\) mA/V², \(V_T(300)=0{,}7\) V, \(dV_T/dT=-2\) mV/K, \(\mu\propto T^{-1{,}5}\). \(V_{GS}=2{,}5\) V va 1,0 V da to‘yinish tokini 300 K va 400 K da toping.

Yechim

\(k(400)=4\cdot(4/3)^{-1{,}5}=2{,}60\) mA/V², \(V_T(400)=0{,}5\) V. \(V_{GS}=2{,}5\): 6,48 → 5,20 mA (kamayadi). \(V_{GS}=1{,}0\): 0,18 → 0,325 mA (ortadi). ZTC nuqtasi ular orasida.

4Darsda mustaqil yechish uchun masalalar

  1. Quvvat MOSFET: \(P=10\) Vt, \(R_{jc}=1{,}5\), \(R_{cs}=0{,}5\), \(R_{sa}=3\) K/Vt, \(T_a=25\) °C. \(T_j\) = ?
    Javob

    75 °C

  2. \(d_{ox}=2\) nm uchun \(V_{bd}\) (10 MV/sm) = ?
    Javob

    2 V

  3. Simulyatorda \(V_{GS}\) ni o‘zgartirib, 300 va 400 K da tok deyarli teng bo‘ladigan \(V_{GS}\) ni toping.
    Javob

    ≈ 1,6–1,8 V (model parametrlariga bog‘liq)

  4. Nima uchun quvvat MOSFETlarni parallel ulash BTlarga qaraganda xavfsizroq (katta \(V_{GS}\) da)?
    Javob

    Tok harorat bilan kamayadi — issiq nuqtaga tok to‘planmaydi

6Kompyuterda modellashtirish

Python (Colab/Jupyter) — nusxalab ishga tushiring va natijani qo‘lda hisob bilan solishtiring (TN6, TN16, TN17).

import numpy as np
def Id(Vgs, T, k300=4e-3, Vt300=0.7):
    k = k300*(T/300)**-1.5; Vt = Vt300 - 2e-3*(T-300)
    return 0.5*k*max(Vgs-Vt, 0)**2
for Vgs in np.arange(0.8, 3.01, 0.2):
    print(f"Vgs={Vgs:.1f}  Id300={Id(Vgs,300)*1e3:6.2f} mA  Id400={Id(Vgs,400)*1e3:6.2f} mA")
