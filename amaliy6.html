1Mashg‘ulot maqsadi

Sirt holatlari, sirt sochilishi va oksid zaryadining harakatchanlik, bo‘sag‘a kuchlanishi va subbo‘sag‘a qiyalikka ta’sirini hisoblash; MDYa tranzistorlar turlarini tasniflash.

Nazariy asos: 10-ma’ruza. Ta’lim natijalari: TN3, TN4, TN13.

Kerakli vositalarKalkulyator yoki Python (Colab), ngspice/LTspice, darslikdagi interaktiv model, tegishli datasheet.

2Qisqacha nazariy asos

Effektiv harakatchanlik vertikal maydon ortishi bilan kamayadi: \(\mu_{eff}=\dfrac{\mu_0}{1+\theta(V_{GS}-V_T)}\). Sirt holatlari subbo‘sag‘a qiyalikni oshiradi: \(S=\ln10\frac{kT}{q}\left(1+\frac{C_d+qD_{it}}{C_{ox}}\right)\). To‘rt tur: n-kanal boyitish (\(V_T>0\)), n-kanal kambag‘allashtirish (\(V_T\lt 0\)), p-kanal boyitish (\(V_T\lt 0\)), p-kanal kambag‘allashtirish (\(V_T>0\)).

3Ishlangan masalalar

1-masala. Harakatchanlik degradatsiyasi

Shart. \(\mu_0=600\) sm²/(V·s), \(\theta=0{,}2\) V⁻¹, \(V_{GS}-V_T=2\) V. \(\mu_{eff}\) = ?

Yechim

\(\mu_{eff}=600/1{,}4\approx429\) sm²/(V·s).

2-masala. Sirt holatlarining S ga ta’siri

Shart. \(C_{ox}=345\) nF/sm², \(C_d=100\) nF/sm², \(D_{it}=10^{11}\) sm⁻²eV⁻¹, 300 K. S = ?

Yechim

\(qD_{it}=16\) nF/sm². \(S=59{,}5(1+116/345)\approx79{,}5\) mV/dek. \(D_{it}=0\) da 76,7 mV/dek.

4Darsda mustaqil yechish uchun masalalar

  1. \(D_{it}=10^{12}\) sm⁻²eV⁻¹ bo‘lsa, yuqoridagi misolda S = ?
    Javob

    ≈ 104 mV/dek

  2. Qaysi MDYa turi “normal-ochiq”?
    Javob

    kambag‘allashtirish (qurilgan kanalli)

  3. \(\theta=0{,}5\) V⁻¹ da \(V_{ov}=1\) V va 3 V uchun \(\mu_{eff}/\mu_0\) = ?
    Javob

    0,67 va 0,4

  4. Belgilarda boyitish va kambag‘allashtirish turlari qanday farqlanadi?
    Javob

    uzuq/uzluksiz kanal chizig‘i

5Interaktiv model: MOSFET xarakteristikalari

6Kompyuterda modellashtirish

Python (Colab/Jupyter) — nusxalab ishga tushiring va natijani qo‘lda hisob bilan solishtiring (TN6, TN16, TN17).

import numpy as np
kT = 0.02585; q = 1.602e-19
Cox, Cd = 345e-9, 100e-9
for Dit in [0, 1e10, 1e11, 1e12]:
    S = np.log(10)*kT*(1 + (Cd + q*Dit)/Cox)*1e3
    print(f"Dit={Dit:.0e} cm^-2 eV^-1  ->  S = {S:.1f} mV/dec")
