1Mashg‘ulot maqsadi

MDYa strukturaning C–V xarakteristikasidan oksid qalinligi, taglik legirlanishi, tekis zonalar kuchlanishi, oksid zaryadi va sirt holatlari zichligini aniqlashni o‘rganish.

Nazariy asos: 10-ma’ruza. Ta’lim natijalari: TN4, TN13, TN17.

Kerakli vositalarKalkulyator yoki Python (Colab), ngspice/LTspice, darslikdagi interaktiv model, tegishli datasheet.

2Qisqacha nazariy asos

Asosiy munosabatlar: \(C_{ox}=\varepsilon_{ox}/d_{ox}\); \(V_{FB}=\varphi_{ms}-Q_f/C_{ox}\); yuqori chastotali minimal sig‘im \(C_{min}=\left(\frac1{C_{ox}}+\frac{W_{max}}{\varepsilon_s}\right)^{-1}\), \(W_{max}=\sqrt{\frac{4\varepsilon_s\varphi_F}{qN_a}}\). Sirt holatlari zichligi past va yuqori chastotali egri chiziqlar farqidan:

$$qD_{it}=\left(\frac{1}{C_{LF}}-\frac{1}{C_{ox}}\right)^{-1}-\left(\frac{1}{C_{HF}}-\frac{1}{C_{ox}}\right)^{-1}$$

Harakatchan ionlar zaryadi “zo‘riqish ostida qizdirish” (BTS) sinovida \(V_{FB}\) siljishidan: \(N_m=C_{ox}\Delta V_{FB}/q\).

3Ishlangan masalalar

1-masala. Oksid qalinligi va qo‘zg‘almas zaryad

Shart. Zatvor yuzasi \(A=10^{-3}\) sm², akkumulyatsiyadagi sig‘im \(C_{max}=345\) pF, ideal \(V_{FB}=-0{,}9\) V, o‘lchangan \(V_{FB}=-1{,}2\) V. \(d_{ox}\) va \(Q_f/q\) ni toping.

Yechim

\(C_{ox}=345\ \text{pF}/10^{-3}\ \text{sm}^2=3{,}45\cdot10^{-7}\) F/sm² ⇒ \(d_{ox}=3{,}45\cdot10^{-13}/3{,}45\cdot10^{-7}=10\) nm. \(\Delta V_{FB}=-0{,}3\) V ⇒ \(Q_f/q=0{,}3\cdot3{,}45\cdot10^{-7}/1{,}602\cdot10^{-19}\approx6{,}5\cdot10^{11}\) sm⁻² (musbat).

2-masala. Sirt holatlari zichligi

Shart. Biror \(V_G\) da \(C_{ox}=345\), \(C_{LF}=250\), \(C_{HF}=200\) nF/sm². \(D_{it}\) ni toping.

Yechim

\((1/250-1/345)^{-1}=908\) nF/sm²; \((1/200-1/345)^{-1}=476\) nF/sm². \(qD_{it}=432\) nF/sm² ⇒ \(D_{it}=4{,}32\cdot10^{-7}/1{,}602\cdot10^{-19}\approx2{,}7\cdot10^{12}\) sm⁻²eV⁻¹ (sifati past chegara).

4Darsda mustaqil yechish uchun masalalar

  1. \(d_{ox}=20\) nm SiO₂ uchun \(C_{ox}\) ni nF/sm² da toping.
    Javob

    172,5 nF/sm²

  2. BTS sinovida \(d_{ox}=50\) nm, \(\Delta V_{FB}=-0{,}15\) V. Harakatchan ionlar zichligi \(N_m\) = ?
    Javob

    \(C_{ox}=69\) nF/sm² ⇒ \(N_m\approx6{,}5\cdot10^{10}\) sm⁻²

  3. p-Si, \(N_a=10^{16}\) sm⁻³ uchun \(\varphi_F\) va \(W_{max}\) ni toping (\(n_i=10^{10}\)).
    Javob

    \(\varphi_F=0{,}357\) V, \(W_{max}\approx0{,}30\) mkm

  4. Simulyatorda \(N_a=10^{16}\), \(d_{ox}=50\) nm o‘rnatib \(C_{min}/C_{ox}\) ni o‘qing va qo‘lda hisob bilan solishtiring.
    Javob

    ≈ 0,2–0,25 (simulyator qiymati bilan tekshiriladi)

5Interaktiv model: MDYa C–V

6Kompyuterda modellashtirish

Python (Colab/Jupyter) — nusxalab ishga tushiring va natijani qo‘lda hisob bilan solishtiring (TN6, TN16, TN17).

import numpy as np
q, eps_s, eps_ox, kT = 1.602e-19, 11.7*8.854e-14, 3.9*8.854e-14, 0.02585
Na, dox, Vfb, ni = 1e17, 10e-7, -0.9, 1e10
Cox = eps_ox/dox; phiF = kT*np.log(Na/ni); gam = np.sqrt(2*q*eps_s*Na)/Cox
VT = Vfb + 2*phiF + gam*np.sqrt(2*phiF)
def C_hf(V):
    if V <= Vfb: return Cox
    if V >= VT:  ps = 2*phiF
    else:        ps = ((-gam + np.sqrt(gam**2 + 4*(V - Vfb)))/2)**2
    W = np.sqrt(2*eps_s*ps/(q*Na)); return 1/(1/Cox + W/eps_s)
for V in np.linspace(-3, 3, 13): print(f"{V:5.1f} V  C/Cox = {C_hf(V)/Cox:.3f}")
print("VT =", round(VT, 3), "V")

8Darsdagi faollikni baholash (JN tarkibida 20 ball)

DarajaBall (20 dan)Mezon (fan dasturi bo‘yicha)
Yuqori18–20Masalaning fizik mohiyatini to‘liq tushunadi, kerakli qonuniyatlarni (Puasson, uzluksizlik tenglamalari) aniq tanlaydi, tashuvchilar o‘tkazuvchanligini bexato hisoblab, mustaqil yechish algoritmini tuzadi.
Yetarli14–17Shartni tushunadi va asosiy qonuniyatlarni yozadi, biroq murakkab sxemalarni (MDYa tranzistorlar) hisoblashda yoki Q-nuqtani topishda o‘qituvchi yordamiga tayanadi.
Minimal12–13Faqat bitta formulaga qo‘yiladigan sodda masalalarni yozadi; VAX chizmalarini o‘qiy olmaydi, qonuniyatlarni tanlashda adashadi.
Erishilmagan0–11Masala shartini tushunmaydi, asboblar fizikasini amaliyotga bog‘lay olmaydi, zonaviy diagrammalarni chiza olmaydi.

Darsdagi faollik (DF) butun semestr davomidagi amaliy mashg‘ulotlar bo‘yicha jami 20 ball bilan baholanadi. Ball oraliqlari fanning umumiy foiz chegaralari (90/70/60 %) asosida keltirilgan. Rasmiy ball HEMIS’da qo‘yiladi.
