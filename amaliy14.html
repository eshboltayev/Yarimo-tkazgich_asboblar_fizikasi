1Mashg‘ulot maqsadi

Diffuziyalangan yoki implantatsiyalangan notekis qatlamning qatlam (varaq) qarshiligini, o‘rtacha solishtirma qarshiligini va to‘rt zondli o‘lchov natijalarini hisoblash.

Nazariy asos: 1-ma’ruza. Ta’lim natijalari: TN4, TN5, TN10, TN13.

Kerakli vositalarKalkulyator yoki Python (Colab), ngspice/LTspice, darslikdagi interaktiv model, tegishli datasheet.

2Qisqacha nazariy asos

Notekis legirlangan qatlam uchun qatlam qarshiligi:

$$R_\square=\left[q\int_0^{x_j}\mu(N(x))\,N(x)\,dx\right]^{-1},\qquad \bar\rho=R_\square\,x_j$$

To‘rt zondli usul (yupqa qatlam, katta namuna): \(R_\square=\dfrac{\pi}{\ln2}\dfrac{V}{I}\approx4{,}532\dfrac{V}{I}\); qalin namuna uchun \(\rho=2\pi s\dfrac{V}{I}\) (\(s\) — zondlar oralig‘i). Harakatchanlik konsentratsiyaga bog‘liq (Caughey–Thomas): \(\mu_n=65+\dfrac{1265}{1+(N/8{,}5\cdot10^{16})^{0{,}72}}\) sm²/(V·s). Diffuziya profillari: cheklanmagan manba — \(\text{erfc}\), cheklangan manba — Gauss.

3Ishlangan masalalar

1-masala. To‘rt zondli o‘lchov

Shart. \(V/I=10\) Ω, \(x_j=1\) mkm. \(R_\square\) va \(\bar\rho\) = ?

Yechim

\(R_\square=45{,}3\) Ω/□; \(\bar\rho=45{,}3\cdot10^{-4}=4{,}5\cdot10^{-3}\) Ω·sm.

2-masala. Bir jinsli qatlam bilan tekshirish

Shart. n-qatlam \(N_d=10^{18}\) sm⁻³, qalinligi 2 mkm. \(\mu_n\), \(\rho\), \(R_\square\) = ?

Yechim

\((10^{18}/8{,}5\cdot10^{16})^{0{,}72}\approx5{,}9\) ⇒ \(\mu_n\approx248\) sm²/(V·s); \(\rho=1/(1{,}602\cdot10^{-19}\cdot10^{18}\cdot248)\approx0{,}025\) Ω·sm; \(R_\square\approx126\) Ω/□.

4Darsda mustaqil yechish uchun masalalar

  1. \(R_\square=200\) Ω/□ bo‘lgan qatlamda to‘rt zondli o‘lchovda \(I=1\) mA. \(V\) = ?
    Javob

    ≈ 44 mV

  2. Qalin namuna, \(s=1\) mm, \(V/I=5\) Ω. \(\rho\) = ?
    Javob

    ≈ 3,14 Ω·sm

  3. Simulyatorda \(N_s=3\cdot10^{19}\), \(L_d=1\) mkm da \(x_j\) va \(R_\square\) ni o‘qing; \(L_d\) 2 marta oshganda R qanday o‘zgaradi?
    Javob

    x_j ≈ 3,2 mkm, R_□ ≈ 24 Ω/□; L_d 2 marta oshsa, x_j 2 marta ortadi, R_□ ≈ 2 marta kamayadi

  4. Nima uchun \(\bar\rho\) dan topilgan “o‘rtacha konsentratsiya” sirtdagidan kichik?
    Javob

    profil chuqurlik bo‘yicha kamayadi

5Interaktiv model: Qatlam qarshiligi

6Kompyuterda modellashtirish

Python (Colab/Jupyter) — nusxalab ishga tushiring va natijani qo‘lda hisob bilan solishtiring (TN6, TN16, TN17).

import numpy as np
q = 1.602e-19
mu = lambda N: 65 + 1265/(1 + (N/8.5e16)**0.72)
Ns, Ld, Nb = 3e19, 1e-4, 1e15          # sm^-3, sm, sm^-3
xj = Ld*np.sqrt(np.log(Ns/Nb))
x = np.linspace(0, xj, 2000); N = Ns*np.exp(-(x/Ld)**2) - Nb
Rs = 1/np.trapz(q*mu(N)*N, x)
print(f"x_j = {xj*1e4:.2f} um, R_sheet = {Rs:.1f} Ohm/sq, V/I (4-zond) = {Rs/4.532:.2f} Ohm")

DarajaBall (20 dan)Mezon (fan dasturi bo‘yicha)
Yuqori18–20Masalaning fizik mohiyatini to‘liq tushunadi, kerakli qonuniyatlarni (Puasson, uzluksizlik tenglamalari) aniq tanlaydi, tashuvchilar o‘tkazuvchanligini bexato hisoblab, mustaqil yechish algoritmini tuzadi.
Yetarli14–17Shartni tushunadi va asosiy qonuniyatlarni yozadi, biroq murakkab sxemalarni (MDYa tranzistorlar) hisoblashda yoki Q-nuqtani topishda o‘qituvchi yordamiga tayanadi.
Minimal12–13Faqat bitta formulaga qo‘yiladigan sodda masalalarni yozadi; VAX chizmalarini o‘qiy olmaydi, qonuniyatlarni tanlashda adashadi.
