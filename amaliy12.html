1Mashg‘ulot maqsadi

Puasson tenglamasidan keskin va chiziqli o‘tishlar uchun to‘siq sig‘imi formulalarini keltirib chiqarish va kuchlanishga bog‘liqligini hisoblash.

Nazariy asos: 3-ma’ruza. Ta’lim natijalari: TN4, TN13, TN17.

Kerakli vositalarKalkulyator yoki Python (Colab), ngspice/LTspice, darslikdagi interaktiv model, tegishli datasheet.

2Qisqacha nazariy asos

Umumiy ta’rif — differensial sig‘im: \(C=\dfrac{dQ}{dV}=\dfrac{\varepsilon_sA}{W}\). Keskin o‘tish: \(W=\sqrt{\dfrac{2\varepsilon_s(V_{bi}-V)}{qN_{eff}}}\), \(N_{eff}=\dfrac{N_aN_d}{N_a+N_d}\) ⇒ \(C\propto(V_{bi}-V)^{-1/2}\). Chiziqli o‘tish (gradiyent \(a\), sm⁻⁴): \(W=\left(\dfrac{12\varepsilon_s(V_{bi}-V)}{qa}\right)^{1/3}\) ⇒ \(C\propto(V_{bi}-V)^{-1/3}\). Umumiy holda \(C\propto(V_{bi}-V)^{-m}\).

3Ishlangan masalalar

1-masala. Keskin p⁺-n o‘tish sig‘imi

Shart. \(N_d=10^{16}\) sm⁻³, \(A=10^{-3}\) sm², \(V_{bi}=0{,}8\) V, \(V=-5\) V. \(W\) va \(C\) = ?

Yechim

\(W=\sqrt{2\cdot1{,}036\cdot10^{-12}\cdot5{,}8/(1{,}602\cdot10^{-19}\cdot10^{16})}\approx0{,}87\) mkm; \(C=1{,}036\cdot10^{-12}\cdot10^{-3}/8{,}66\cdot10^{-5}\approx12{,}0\) pF.

2-masala. Chiziqli o‘tish

Shart. \(a=10^{20}\) sm⁻⁴, \(V_{bi}-V=5\) V, \(A=10^{-3}\) sm². \(W\), \(C\) = ?

Yechim

\(W=(12\cdot1{,}036\cdot10^{-12}\cdot5/(1{,}602\cdot10^{-19}\cdot10^{20}))^{1/3}=(3{,}88\cdot10^{-12})^{1/3}\approx1{,}57\cdot10^{-4}\) sm; \(C\approx6{,}6\) pF.

4Darsda mustaqil yechish uchun masalalar

  1. Misol 1 da \(V=0\) bo‘lsa, C = ?
    Javob

    ≈ 32 pF

  2. Keskin o‘tishda teskari kuchlanish 0 dan −8 V ga o‘zgarsa (\(V_{bi}=0{,}8\) V), sig‘im necha marta kamayadi?
    Javob

    √11 ≈ 3,3 marta

  3. Varikapning sozlash koeffitsiyenti \(C_{max}/C_{min}\) ni oshirish uchun qanday profil kerak?
    Javob

    giperkeskin (m > 1/2)

  4. Simulyatorda \(V\) ni o‘zgartirib, \(C_j\) ning \((V_{bi}-V)^{-1/2}\) qonuniyatini 3 nuqtada tekshiring.
    Javob

    C·√(V_bi − V) ≈ const

6Kompyuterda modellashtirish

Python (Colab/Jupyter) — nusxalab ishga tushiring va natijani qo‘lda hisob bilan solishtiring (TN6, TN16, TN17).

import numpy as np
q, eps, A = 1.602e-19, 1.036e-12, 1e-3
Nd, Vbi = 1e16, 0.8
for V in [0, -1, -2, -5, -10]:
    W = np.sqrt(2*eps*(Vbi - V)/(q*Nd)); C = eps*A/W
    print(f"V={V:4} V  W={W*1e4:.3f} um  C={C*1e12:6.2f} pF  1/C^2={1/C**2:.3e} F^-2")
